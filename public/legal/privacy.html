Privacy Policy

Last updated: 25 November 2026

This policy explains what {{BUSINESS_NAME}} (“we”) does with your information when you use wemakesucess.

Your resume

If you do not sign in, your resume is saved only in your own browser (local storage) so you can come back to it. If you sign in (with an email link or Google), your email address, name and your documents, including any photo you add and proof of your purchases, are stored in your account with our database provider Supabase so you can open them on any device. Signing out removes the copies from that browser. When you download a paid PDF, your resume is sent to our server only to generate the PDF and is not kept afterwards.

Payments

If you use the AI writing buttons, the text needed for that suggestion (your target job title, job titles and companies, skills and the text you ask to improve) is sent to Anthropic to generate suggestions. We do not send your name or contact details, and Anthropic does not use API data to train its models.

Payments are handled by Razorpay. We pass your name, email and phone number from your resume to Razorpay to pre-fill the checkout. We never see or store your card, UPI or bank details. We receive the order and payment ids, which we use to verify and restore your purchase.

Technical data

Our hosting provider, Cloudflare, processes standard request data such as IP addresses to deliver and protect the site. The interface fonts load from Google Fonts, which receives your IP address. We do not use advertising or tracking cookies.

Sharing

We do not sell your data. We share it only with the providers named above, to run the service, or when the law requires it.

Your choices

You can delete a resume at any time from My Documents; when you are signed in this also removes it from your account. To delete your account and everything in it, write to {{SUPPORT_EMAIL}}. Without an account, clearing this site's data in your browser deletes your resumes. For questions or requests about your data, write to {{SUPPORT_EMAIL}}.

Changes

We may update this policy; the date above shows the latest version.
