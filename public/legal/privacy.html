This policy explains what {{BUSINESS_NAME}} (“we”) does with your information when you use wemakesucess.

Your resume

Your resume is saved in your own browser (local storage) so you can come back to it. It is not stored on our servers. When you download a paid PDF, your resume is sent to our server only to generate the PDF and is not kept afterwards.

Payments

Payments are handled by Razorpay. We pass your name, email and phone number from your resume to Razorpay to pre-fill the checkout. We never see or store your card, UPI or bank details. We receive the order and payment ids, which we use to verify and restore your purchase.

Technical data

Our hosting provider, Cloudflare, processes standard request data such as IP addresses to deliver and protect the site. The interface fonts load from Google Fonts, which receives your IP address. We do not use advertising or tracking cookies.

Sharing

We do not sell your data. We share it only with the providers named above, to run the service, or when the law requires it.

Your choices

You can delete your resume at any time by clearing this site's data in your browser. For questions or requests about your data, write to {{SUPPORT_EMAIL}}.

Changes

We may update this policy; the date above shows the latest version.
