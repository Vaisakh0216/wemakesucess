Cancellation and Refund Policy

Last updated: 25 November 2026

wemakesucess sells a digital product: a one-time ₹299 unlock to download one resume as a PDF, and a one-time ₹99 unlock to download one cover letter as a PDF. You can build and preview your full resume or cover letter for free before paying, so please check the preview before you buy.

When we refund

  • You were charged but could not download your PDF, and we could not fix it within 7 days of your request.
  • You were charged more than once for the same resume.
  • The payment was made without your authorisation.

Because the PDF is delivered instantly and can be downloaded any number of times, we do not offer refunds for a change of mind once a PDF has been downloaded.

Cancellation

There is no subscription to cancel. Each payment is a one-time purchase.

How to request a refund

Email {{SUPPORT_EMAIL}} within 7 days of payment with your Razorpay order id and a short description of the problem. Approved refunds go back to your original payment method within 5 to 7 business days.
